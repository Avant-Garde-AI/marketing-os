## Motion direction: an event, with consequences

Give each motion instance one observable event and a small set of named motion
subjects grounded in the source pixels. Separate:

| Decision | Contract for a candidate |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Verified unframed master, digest and inspected visual inventory. Full-work reveal must retain the complete composition. |
| Event | One concrete verb and causal order: a gust reaches the foreground, then the middle distance; a wave travels across the scene. |
| Movement | Named objects, direction, relative displacement, timing and settling. Proposed displacement is a directing target, not an automatically verified result. |
| Camera | Choose fixed camera for artwork animation. A crop/reveal or matched cut belongs to an explicitly different editorial expression. Camera movement alone cannot pass an object-motion brief. |
| Protected regions | Signature, text, frame boundary if any, rigid architecture and nonmoving faces/figures stay stable. Reject unwanted redraw, new anatomy, new objects or smeared detail. |
| Return | Opening and closing pose, plus velocity through the seam. Matching end images do not establish matching velocity or an invisible loop. |
| Display | Inspect muted, at phone size and full speed; inspect slowed frames for fidelity and repeated seam playback. |

For an illustrative five-to-eight-second candidate: action begins within the
first second; the middle has a clearly different state and ordered movement;
the final interval restores a stable composition. Use one request spanning the
whole temporal script when the chosen model supports it. Three narrative beats
do not automatically mean three provider requests. Confirm current capabilities,
duration, resolution and exact price through the connected provider before
dispatch. Do not reuse a historical credit quote.

### Acceptance and repair

Compare the candidate with a same-artwork control before claiming improvement.
The October 10 and 16 outputs differ in artwork and age, so they are diagnostic
examples, not an experiment demonstrating lift.

Record human decisions for visible motion, exact-work fidelity, an earned middle
beat, seam continuity and mobile readability against the output digest. A reject
names the region/interval and a scoped repair. Keep the rejected result. Hold
failed output outside the final post binding. Diagnostics can flag low change;
global brightness, camera shake, a cut or morphing can create high change without
acceptable animation. Neither the existing 0.25% diagnostic nor a proposed
displacement target grants creative approval.

A bounded pilot should compare the existing model's stronger event brief with a
first/last-frame-capable candidate on the same artwork, if available. Review at
most two initial outputs, retain both, and stop for a scoped repair decision
instead of automatic rerolls. Generation quote/budget authorization and final
publish/schedule consent remain separate existing controls.

## Two additional narrative families

### Detail → changed reading

Start on an ordinary-looking, explicitly derived crop. Pull back or change the
view to expose context that changes the first interpretation. Finish on the exact
whole work. The reveal must add a concrete fact visible in the source; a random
zoom does not qualify. A still carousel and a deterministic animated crop are
different expressions of the same story. The moving crop is editorial motion,
not evidence that objects inside the artwork were animated.

Inspect the opening viewport, not just its center: it must withhold the revealing
context without cutting faces or making the artwork misleading. Require adequate
native source resolution for any close crop; a low-resolution study cannot be
upscaled and called a production detail. No invented painting process or
artist-intent claim. This family already exists as a draft Arthaus concept; build
on it rather than create a duplicate.

### Visual rhyme → surprising relationship

Hold a visible motif in work A; make a deliberate matched cut to a related motif
in work B; reveal both complete works so the reader understands the relationship.
The payoff is learning how to see or pair two works. It differs from three
flattering rooms or a slideshow of similarly tagged products.

Graph/catalog retrieval proposes candidates. Source inspection must confirm the
relationship, motif coordinates, compatible crop and truthful credit. A graph
similarity score is not enough. Render from exact source pixels; do not morph
one artist's work into another or assert influence/collaboration. If the visual
match needs excessive stretching, crop loss or explanatory prose, reject that
pair. The full pair can close as a still; this expression need not be seamless.
