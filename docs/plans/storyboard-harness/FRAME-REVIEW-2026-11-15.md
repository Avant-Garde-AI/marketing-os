# Physical frame review: detail and complete borders

The November 21–December 4 Arthaus batch reached nine scheduled posts and five
automatic carousel holds. Three rejected JPEGs passed every physical criterion
at confidence 0.85. Two had a real defect: a strong top bevel was selected as the
outer frame boundary, leaving the original top border above the refitted frame.
The failed render hashes were reproduced from existing paid results before any
change. These are operational observations, not evidence of engagement.

The platform measures candidate borders jointly for the near-frontal wooden
frame recipe. An isolated border width outside 0.65–1.7 times the median of the
four strongest candidates must have a consistent measured alternative or hold.
Every edge still requires at least 95% sample agreement. This distinguishes an
inner decorative groove from the material boundary without generating images.

New delivery receipts explicitly select `reframe.mode: expand-only-v2`. Wider
art expands the frame horizontally; narrower art expands it vertically. Neither
shrinks the frame and leaves old border pixels behind. The full artwork remains
contained without cropping. Absent mode retains the legacy transform exactly;
existing reviewed pixels and schedule hashes must not change.

`frame-fit-v3` reviews the full final JPEG plus a magnified crop derived from the
same bytes. The runtime supplies geometry-derived bounds containing the original
and fitted frame, with wall padding. The platform validates the full SHA and
bounds, derives the crop, and records its SHA. All four physical booleans and
confidence at least 0.90 remain mandatory. V1 retains 0.94; V2 retains 0.90.
Confidence is an operational model response, not a calibrated probability.

This is a physical compositing check. It does not implement persona/taste review,
creative effectiveness or narrative admission. `packages/storyboard` imports no
renderer. Generic geometry, delivery validation and proof bounds live in the
open-core template; paid execution and recovery authority live in the platform;
Arthaus recipes, masters and production artifacts live in its store repo.

Recovery requires the unchanged active standing grant, recorded V2 rejections,
successful existing jobs, full review lead and no manual hold. It archives prior
transforms and checks once, retains successful child renders and existing
schedules, and renews the Slack notice before scheduling additions. A recorded
negative physical criterion requires a changed final JPEG before V3 review.
Confidence-only rejection may enter the new detail-aware contract once. V3
rejections cannot be reset by this migration. Provider generations are never
resubmitted. Runtime profile advertisement prevents execution against old code.

Verification covers the top-bevel defect, expansion without residual frames,
bounded exact-pixel crops, unchanged acceptance thresholds, legacy contracts,
manual holds, successful child retention and no repeated paid submission. Live
completion counts belong in the store's operational receipt after execution.

## Correction after the production V3 run

The first repair completed on November 15 with nine scheduled and five held;
it was not a successful backlog repair. V3 fixed the two residual top borders,
but three subsequent checks still marked every physical criterion true at 0.85.
A 0.90 self-reported score has no calibrated interpretation. The full-resolution
comparison also exposed a second compositor defect: after fitting the frame to
the artwork ratio, a second `contain` resize used the longest trapezoid edges
and introduced pale bands into the artwork texture.

`full-source-v3` projects the complete original artwork texture into the fitted
opening. A proportional downsample prefilter prevents aliasing; it adds no mat
and crops no source edge. Existing receipts retain their exact raster path.
The frame expands as in V2, preserving the original outside border.

`frame-fit-v4` requires a valid response, explicit `inspectionComplete: true`,
and all four physical criteria true. An unjudgeable criterion must be false.
It still requires measured geometry (at least 0.95 agreement), the exact final
JPEG hash, full-scene context and a geometry-bound crop. Confidence remains
recorded in [0,1] as telemetry, not a calibrated acceptance threshold. This
corrects the earlier numerical gate; legacy V1/V2/V3 receipt thresholds remain
unchanged. This is not evidence of creative effectiveness.

The source-fit migration is separate and bounded: only recorded V3 failures
with `expand-only-v2` transforms can enter once. It archives their transforms
and checks, preserves passing children, schedules and manual holds, and requires
a changed final JPEG before any new review, including uncertainty-only failures.
V4/full-source failures do not qualify. No paid generation is resubmitted.
The platform checks the runtime advertises both V4 and the full-source mode.
New additions require a renewed Slack notice and the full review lead.

## Independent material boundaries

The source-fit run recovered four carousels and scheduled thirteen posts; the
last December 4 space image still failed V4's physical checks. Its saved
background showed that the detector forced the outer rim to share the inner
bevel's slope. These boundaries can have different slopes and border widths.
Independent outer measurements remove this corner mismatch without changing
the renderer or weakening contrast, 95% sample agreement, enclosure or review.
All 21 saved backgrounds remained measurable under the correction.

The platform tags new measurements `frame-pixel-v3`. A separate bounded repair
accepts only an unrealized V4 edge rejection from the old parallel-border
profile with two passing siblings. It requires changed measured outer geometry,
the same provider background and a changed final JPEG before another V4 check.
Prior evidence is archived; existing schedules, owner holds and paid jobs remain
intact. New-profile failures stay held. New additions need a fresh review notice.

## Blank-pane raster halos

Original-scene evidence cleared the empty-frame and artwork-spill flags, while
the final image still failed fit and edge plausibility. The verified 1440×1920
Space Runner master has no pale border. The sliver comes from original neutral
pane pixels just outside the projected artwork quad after frame resampling.
It must be corrected in pixels, not waived by another same-input review.

`pane-bleed-v4` preserves the full-source projection and all original corner
features. It extends clamped source-edge colours at most four output pixels
outside the artwork quad, only into bright neutral pane pixels inside the
measured outer rim. Dark/coloured frame material and the surrounding scene are
untouched. It does not resize the artwork to an expanded quad or crop its edges.
This is deliberately limited to the existing near-frontal dark wooden frame /
neutral blank pane recipe. Earlier renderer modes retain their exact pixels.

A bounded old-renderer halo repair may change only that mode for the failed
child, retaining source and geometry and preserving its two successful siblings.
The new final JPEG must change and must receive the same reference-aware V4
physical check. It does not re-enter the evidence-only migration. A new-mode
failure stays held. Runtime capability advertisement prevents execution before
the updated template is deployed. No paid generation is submitted.
