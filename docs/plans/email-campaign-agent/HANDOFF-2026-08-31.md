## Next workstreams

Ordered by what unblocks the most. E-series is email refinement; P-series is
platform hygiene.

### E1 — Campaign creation & tuning loop *(the priority)*

The pieces exist; the *flow* between them has never been exercised as a loop.
Today a session builds a campaign, mints a link, and stops. The intended cycle
is: build → share → notes come back → **revise from the notes** → re-share.

The revise step has never run. Specifically:

- `email_review_notes` returns notes but no tool consumes them into a revision.
  Does the agent re-author from a note, or propose a diff a human accepts?
- A revised campaign should show reviewers *what changed*. The room shows
  current state only; there is no version history and no "since you last
  looked".
- Notes are per-campaign or per-`slot`. Nothing anchors a note to a *block*, so
  "the third product image is wrong" has to be said in prose.
- The sheet shows open-note counts; there is no cross-campaign worklist for
  "what came back from this review round".
