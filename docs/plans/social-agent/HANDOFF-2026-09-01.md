**P1 landed.** `social/reference/genome.md` now carries two archetypes with
counted evidence — `n=32` and `n=20` — above the five brand-derived ones that
still say `n=0`. `social/reference/scripts/emit-genome.mjs` is the deterministic
canonicals→genome route; it refuses to run on a mock corpus, never overwrites
brand-derived archetypes, and does not inflate a count. Verified with the
platform's own parser: parses, and all seven resolve in-bounds at 1080×1080,
1350 and 1920.

**Read the funnel honestly before leaning on those numbers:** 261 extracted →
198 traced → **66 quarantined by the S4 judge** → 132 clustered → 39% coverage,
with `min_cluster_size` auto-lowered to 7. Two archetypes is a thin harvest.
The brand-derived five remain the fallback for every role the corpus never
showed, and that is the intended design, not a shortfall.

**The first extraction pass was thrown away, and the reason is worth reading.**
It ran to 261/261 with zero failures against `provider: mock` — `run.yaml`
ships the mock as its default and nothing in a successful run says so. The
output canonicalized cleanly to three clusters at 94% coverage, which is what
made it dangerous: the numbers looked like evidence. Every spec's
`provenance.provider` said `mock`, and each had a single `z0`/`hero` zone where
a real spec has 8–12 named roles. The real run writes to a **separate**
`run-vertex/` tree so the two can never mix.

**Three fixes came out of it**, all in `creative-agent`, and all the same
species — a pipeline that produced confident output nobody could audit:

- **#2, the request timeout.** An unset timeout is "wait forever". One call had
  blocked for 11 hours on 37 seconds of CPU and looked exactly like slow
  progress. Bounded to 180s; per-ad failures are isolated and reported.
- **#3, quarantined parses were being clustered.** S4's judge marks a parse
  `quarantined` when it does not capture the layout — and the pipeline traces
  the ad anyway, so selecting the pool on `trace` fed rejected work straight
  into the archetypes. It was 66 of 198 ads on this run. Bad geometry never
  fails loudly; it just shifts a cluster's median bbox and the archetype still
  looks plausible.
- **#4, the real provider left its artifacts anonymous.** The mock stamped
  `provenance.provider`; Vertex did not. So the obvious lesson from the mock
  incident — *assert the provider before trusting a canonical* — was
  unfollowable, because a real spec had no provider field at all. Now all three
  structured paths stamp provider, model and prompt_version.

The run is also sharded six ways: each ad is ~100s of mostly-network time, so
six workers took the corpus from most of a day to about ninety minutes.

The scratchpad is still the only home for both corpora, but they are no longer
unprotected: `backup-loop.sh` rsyncs to `gs://arthaus-creative-corpus/` every
15 minutes while the jobs run. Confirm a final pass landed before trusting it.

between "we have a plan" and "we have a post":

## Things that are true and easy to get wrong

- **`evidence.n` means "exemplars I counted."** The retail lane counts; the
  artist research lane does not and must never write that field. Three kinds of
  claim — counted, researched, brand-derived — stay separate in the schema
  because conflating them is the failure this whole design guards against.
- **The 12 failed retail handles were fetch failures, not absence of ads.**
  `page_name_seen: []` means the scraper returned nothing. An earlier reading of
  this as "artisanal brands don't advertise" was wrong and has been withdrawn.
  Re-pulling them costs about $0.05.
- **Artists are observable.** Both `apify/instagram-scraper` and
  `apify/facebook-posts-scraper` work on these accounts. The organic corpus also
  removes the paid-vs-organic confound if both cohorts are pulled organically.
- **"Genome" is overloaded.** AMS already calls a per-artist palette a Creative
  Genome; our market reference uses the same word for something else. Rename the
  market one before Easel makes the collision daily.
- **Vercel env types:** *sensitive* vars pull EMPTY though they are set. Only
  *encrypted-and-empty* is a real gap. Check the type over REST before concluding
  a secret is missing.
